How it works

Strip the spaces and punctuation from any paragraph here and the letters read the same in both directions. The text is not edited into shape afterwards; it is a palindrome at every step of its construction.

The generator writes from the middle outward. Each half owes the other a run of letters, its debt. A word added on the right must spell the debt; a word that overshoots flips the leftover letters into a new debt for the left. Words straddle the mirror freely: walk at the start pairs with …k law at the end. Wherever nothing is owed, a palindrome such as racecar can sit in both halves at once.

Punctuation is free, so sentences end where they read best. A literary language model scores every word, a syntax check judges every finished sentence, and repetition and run-ons are priced in. The search keeps the paragraph that reads best of the thousands that close exactly.

Built on Peter Norvig's and Dan Hoey's palindrome search. Source · API
