# Center-out dependency realizer (2026-09-16)

This is a distinct construction route from relation alignment. It realizes
three-clause discourse plans on each side, with a typed topic graph carried
across clauses: an artifact becomes the next clause's topic, then a place
becomes the following topic. Every topic is singular and every finite verb is
agreement-checked. Left and right plans use independent lexical pools.

The bilateral character ledger starts at the seam between the two complete
plans and expands center-out, consuming one matching character obligation at a
time. Complete proposals are rendered and independently audited for exactness;
fragments, word-order mirrors, and catalogue text are never promoted.
