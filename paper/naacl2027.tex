\begin{abstract}
We study paired insertions into a supplied letter-level palindrome. For equal-length insertions, we derive a necessary and sufficient seam equation and verify it against whole-tape reversal. A retrospective lineage grows an exact 568-letter parent to 752 letters in four saved edits: one paired insertion followed by three mirrored-support replacements. A separate bounded clause search yields a 672-letter child. A finite regression tests 54,145 equation cases without mismatch. These results establish replayable exact edits, not readable English: the 752-letter endpoint is rough, and no blinded ratings or language-quality baseline are available.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}

Palindrome generation has been approached through remainder search and n-gram corpus enumeration \citep{norvig2002palindrome,papadopoulos2015palindromes}; constrained text generation also uses lexical decoding and constraint programming \citep{hokamp2017grid,bonlarron-regin-2024}. We study paired local edits to an already exact parent. The contribution is a correctness condition for one insertion operation and an audit of saved constructions, not a general generator or a claim of linguistic quality.

For rendered text $x$, $\norm{x}$ lowercases and keeps only ASCII letters; exactness means $\norm{x}=\rev(\norm{x})$. Write the normalized parent as
\begin{equation}
 p=AqC\rev(q)\rev(A),\qquad C=\rev(C),
\end{equation}
where $q$ is the retained bridge between edit positions. Insert $U$ after $A$ and $V$ after $C$:
\begin{equation}
 p'=AUqCV\rev(q)\rev(A).
\end{equation}
For $|U|=|V|$, the child is a palindrome iff
\begin{equation}
 Uq=q\rev(V). \label{eq:seam}
\end{equation}
After removing the mirrored outer $A$, the two prefixes adjacent to $C$ are $Uq$ and $q\rev(V)$. Their equality also matches the suffix by reversal; the center is already palindromic. Every reported child is additionally checked by two independent full-tape validators.

\section{Results}

\paragraph{Saved lineage.} One selected branch grows from 568 to 752 letters in four edits: one paired insertion, followed by three mirrored-support replacements. Equation~\ref{eq:seam} applies to the insertion only; every descendant is separately checked against the full-tape palindrome condition. Table~\ref{tab:lineage} reports all five audited stages. They are dependent descendants, not independent trials. Repetition statistics are surface diagnostics, not readability scores.

The 752-letter endpoint passes both validators (normalized SHA-256 begins \code{109c758e} and ends \code{f5106f222de3c}). From 686 to 752 letters, distinct token types fall from 56 to 48 and repeated-trigram rate rises from 4.8\% to 6.6\%. The endpoint contains rough clauses and a proper palindromic span; it is a construction result, not a readable example or length record. The full text and replay materials are in the companion evidence archive.

\paragraph{Other checks.} A separate bounded search adds four clauses at two seams of the 568-letter parent, yielding an exact 672-letter child after 9,273 frontier examinations, 35 rejected attempts, and one accepted path. Its inserted clauses are: \emph{Nora stops Leon. Leon stops Nora. Nora stops Noel. Noel spots Nora. Aron stops Leon. Leon spots Aron. Aron spots Noel. Noel spots Aron.} The clauses are schematic and do not establish readability. In a paired-seam ablation, the complete 568-to-630 edit is exact, while either one-sided insertion fails. The equation regression found no mismatch in 54,145 equal-length cases or 91 context cases. Two enumerators produced the same 38,498-candidate finite set; this is an implementation-equivalence check, not a quality or efficiency comparison.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.} The audit is retrospective and selected; most wording is authored, and several outputs share a parent. We collected no reader judgments and ran no language-quality comparison. Human evaluation requires explicit constructs and a reproducible protocol \citep{howcroft-etal-2020-twenty,khashabi-etal-2022-genie}; our mechanical checks cannot substitute for it. Accordingly, the results support exact local editing and replay, not fluent long-form English.
