Bidirectional lexical completion: finite research runs 005 and 006

Outcome
A role-factored bidirectional character grammar now completes words absent from the original fragment bank, without requiring its resulting sentence to occur in that bank. It carries grammatical slots and partial-word offsets throughout the whole palindrome search. Exactness does not rely on individually palindromic sentences. No human-approved new paragraph is claimed.

Implementation and falsifiable checks
The grammar is an acyclic graph of alternative lexical slots. Character arcs carry word, role, offset, grammatical frame and sentence index. Outside-in matching enforces equal mirrored letters; exact graph reachability prunes pairs that cannot bound a grammatical path. These are the only search pruning rules. Token/slot validation and a separate whole-stream exactness check run for every accepted derivation. Full sentences are enumerated only for the comparator, not to admit a product path.

Adversarial tests recover reviled from role-specific prefix re and suffix iled, filling v. Reviled is absent from the original fragment bank and licensed by existing lemma revile plus explicit past/adjectival d. A wrong role rejects the same completion. A wrong final name preserves grammaticality but prevents global palindrome closure. Stressed is completed from str and ssed, filling e, and is licensed by stress+ed. A recipient-food request and affected-human subject can then meet across desserts/stressed. Tests compare the grammar product with exhaustive small fixtures, retain duplicate derivations, include odd centers inside words, and ensure budget exhaustion is never labeled complete.

The concrete new whole-stream fixture is: Noel, deliver mail. Liam reviled Leon. It is30 letters exact; each sentence individually is non-palindromic. This is a grammatical seam, not a cohesive long-paragraph claim. Another fixture is: Noel, deliver Anna desserts. Stressed Anna reviled Leon. It is46 letters exact, with the same Anna as food recipient and affected subject.

Finite controlled results
Every arm had the same limits:20 million method operations,30 seconds,20,000 accepted derivations, and one through four sentences. These operation counters have different costs and cannot establish a broad speedup. All arms completed, with no truncation, no running process, and no input wait. All work was local CPU; no models, paid APIs, remote compute, GPU, publication, merge or paper changes.

005: original116-sentence bank yields12 unique exact texts (3 at60+letters). The expanded248-sentence grammar yields7,636 (3,481 at60+letters). Expanded enumeration and the bidirectional product return identical sets. Enumeration used5,404,797 operations/1.779s; product used79,320/0.773s.
006 adds32 recipient-food and affected-human paths, for280 sentences. Both expanded methods yield14,260 unique exact texts (9,913 at60+letters), again identical. Enumeration used7,336,141 operations/2.368s; product used109,892/1.537s. Original baseline remains12. The largest graph has3,893 nodes.

The yield gain is a vocabulary/grammar coverage effect: the expanded enumerated comparator finds it too. The product removes the prototype's dependency on a full-sentence bank; it does not outperform the same grammar in output quality. All exact outputs remain available, including repeats and copied controls. Deduplication retains derivation counts and raw paths. In these deterministic finite runs each within-arm normalized output has one derivation; duplicates across methods/runs are retained separately. Initial starts/seeds do not randomly prune the search; seeds control sampling only.

Coverage and diversity

Twelve surface forms are absent from the original donor bank. This is a small declared vocabulary, not a claim to cover English. Omitted vocabulary is a coverage limitation, distinct from sound character/reachability pruning.

Seventeen declared grammatical families produce exact closures in nine families: delivery requests, person delivery, food delivery, reference, stopping, past criticism, stressed criticism, and the two old control families.
