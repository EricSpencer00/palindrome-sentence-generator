## Recommendations for paper and audits

1. Preserve the v4 estimate only as a paired, adaptive resource-bundle sensitivity result: 66/120 versus 0/120 basic-valid, with exact strata, selection/gate definitions, and the explicit caveat that beam width, candidate limit, branching and time all changed. Do not say the generator or direction improved, or attribute the coverage difference to any single control.
2. Keep v3's 0/90-per-method outcome as a bounded-configuration observation. Its zero result is neither proof of impossibility nor evidence of parity. Keep v1/v2/development history out of confirmatory denominators.
3. POS pruning has a controlled paired contrast, but the 50-opening study is not backed by a frozen historical source tree. State the limitation. Report accepted totals and denominators; qualify as bounded DFS yield. The four policy studies directly refute traversal invariance and an expansion-efficiency headline. Include per-popped and per-generated rates whenever discussing efficiency.
4. Remove unsourced “30/60 benchmark” comparisons. Define all length strata as inclusive normalized-letter bands. Treat the fixed 38-letter seed as a single target, not an external benchmark or population. Avoid broad traversal-robustness claims; the later 4-policy evidence demonstrates sensitivity.
5. Keep the beam-only study marked exploratory until its actual outcome artifact is audited; its manifest alone is a protocol, not evidence.

## Reproducibility notes

The raw manifests, result JSON and trial JSONL were left unchanged. The POS opening-study audit verifies 100 rows, 50 pairs, all matched accepted rows, and recorded source/input hashes; its provenance says historical source was not frozen. Each later policy study retains its own provenance, summary, raw trials and results. No benchmark registration or external baseline source was found: these are internal frozen controls or fixed targets.
