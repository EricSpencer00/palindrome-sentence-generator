# Discourse-relation involution (2026-09-16)

This bounded diagnostic introduces a distinct construction state: two
independent, semantically linked clauses keep their cause/effect or contrast
relation while their discourse roles are involuted. A live character equation
selects both connective (`because`/`so`, or `although`/`yet`) and subordinate
versus coordinating attachment. It is not active/passive rewriting, paired
lexical grammar, semantic valency, dialogue Q/A, or a larger cross-product;
those registry families are explicitly excluded by preflight.

The run renders 12 complete ordinary-prose candidates from three fresh
proposition pairs. Every candidate was replayed with an opposing-index
two-pointer scan and forward/reverse normalized SHA-256. There were zero exact
closures, so no reader evidence is claimed. Provenance records fresh authored
propositions, no catalogue text, and no word-order mirror. The concrete first
residual repair is to replace the selected connective and reattach the
subordinate clause at that mismatch while preserving relation polarity and
proposition identity.
