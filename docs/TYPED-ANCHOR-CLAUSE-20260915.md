# Typed anchor-clause search (15 September 2026)

This bounded constructive experiment tested whether the requested
semordnilap anchors can support a readable palindrome longer than 38 letters:

`repaid/diaper`, `drawer/reward`, `deliver/reviled`, and
`stressed/desserts`.

The operator enumerated 1,080 complete authored clauses from typed subject,
finite-verb, object, and adverb slots.  Anchor words were required to occur as
whole lexical constituents; the letter comparison therefore naturally crossed
word boundaries when the centre fell inside a constituent.  Candidates were
checked independently with a two-sided normalized-letter audit and the shared
mechanical admission gate.  No wrappers, catalogue text, fragments,
self-palindromic spans, repeated content words, or reused seed sentences were
used.  Every exact hit would have been rendered in the JSON report.

Result: **0 exact admitted hits over 38 letters**.  Consequently this run
produced no reader-worthy output and nothing was promoted to the public
catalogue.  The executable record is
`experiments/typed_anchor_clause_search_20260915.py`.
