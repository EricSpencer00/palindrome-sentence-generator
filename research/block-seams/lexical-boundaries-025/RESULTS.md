A word is tested against the live residual before later words are selected; no complete endpoint phrase list is supplied. The outer generator builds from lexical productions and bound arguments. The main engine continues to add intact phrase chunks; finite repetition cost, payoff guidance and empty-debt center-out closure are unchanged.

Frozen resources are the existing Brown universal POS table (tools/polaris/payload/brown.json.gz), data/lexicon.txt, typed_constituents.VERBS and agreement_morphology.py. Their exact hashes accompany every successful phrase and the summary. The existing environment has no NLTK/WordNet package; no download or external API was used. The current recipes use base forms only. Existing irregular third/past mappings and the morphology transducer are wired into the realizer but not searched in this pilot; their presence does not support a new morphology-coverage claim.

Semantic classes and valency/construction licensing are explicitly owner-authored and recorded in lexical-license.json. Brown POS or dictionary membership alone does not establish referent identity, valency or coherence. Named-entity surfaces come from bound semantic arguments; features must agree with the already committed plan. This remains a limited finite grammar, not a general lexical-semantic parser. It changes the generation mechanism without claiming a comprehensive phrase resource.
