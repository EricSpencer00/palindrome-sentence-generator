# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Eric Spencer's palindrome project, exploring computer-generated
palindromes and reading or downloading finished examples.

## Product Purpose

The repository implements exact letter-level palindrome search. The existing
site lets visitors watch generation. The Panama publication presents the
completed long search result as readable, downloadable text. Its success is
making the construction inspectable and its provenance clear.

## Brand Personality

Playful, direct, experimental. The README and NORTH-STAR distinguish mechanical
success from readable prose; public claims must retain that distinction.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing claims of a world record or coherent prose without evidence.
Avoid a dashboard around what is fundamentally a text to read.

## Design Principles

Show the actual result. Credit Hoey and Norvig. Preserve every letter of the
published artifact. Keep the generator available. Favor a clear reading path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, keyboard-operable navigation and inspection controls,
visible focus, adequate contrast, responsive text, and no required animation.
These are implementation defaults, not a claim of a formal accessibility audit.
