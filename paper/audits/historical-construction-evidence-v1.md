## Evidence boundary and quarantine rationale

The strongest evidence is mechanical: independent normalization of saved `rendered` surfaces, equality against reversal, recomputed lengths and SHA-256 digests, plus explicit run provenance. These establish exactness for the recorded surface. They do not establish linguistic quality. The 54-letter item has an unrun reader packet; for the 38-letter reference, no primary human-rating record, sample size, or protocol was located in the reviewed evidence. The 568/666 and related artifacts also lack located reader-rating records. Brown word-order gain in the Diana appendix is a score against 32 same-word shuffles under one fixed model, not a readability measure; matched Brown controls differ in sentence count and are retrospective. The appendix treats the pair as descriptive and non-causal, but any implication that 38 letters has an established human-readability result should be removed absent its primary source.

Prior release material is explicitly marked quarantined (`paper/EVIDENCE.md`, `paper/DEPOSIT.md`, `paper/SOURCE-AUDIT.md`, and `paper/EVIDENCE-README.md`). The stated reasons include insufficient reader evidence, unrun or failed calibration/control gates, uncertain or inherited provenance, non-independent construction lineages, and historical proxy/baseline claims that were not fit for public promotion. Quarantine is therefore an evidence-status decision; it does not erase the useful narrow fact that selected saved strings are mechanically exact. Conversely, artifact survival alone does not reverse quarantine.
