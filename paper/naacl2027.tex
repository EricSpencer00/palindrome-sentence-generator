\begin{abstract}
This note reports mechanical checks on selected letter-level palindrome constructions from a project archive. For an equal-length paired insertion into an exact parent, a local condition is equivalent to full-tape symmetry. We audit a saved branch from 568 to 752 letters (one insertion followed by three replacements) and replay a bounded 672-letter search. The reported texts pass two exactness checks. These results concern symmetry and replay, not English readability: the prose is rough, and there are no reader ratings or language-quality comparisons.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup and insertion condition}

Letter-level palindrome construction has been approached through remainder search and n-gram corpus enumeration \citep{norvig2002palindrome,papadopoulos2015palindromes}; broader text-generation work includes lexical decoding and constraint programming \citep{hokamp2017grid,bonlarron-regin-2024}. This report is narrower: it records exactness checks for selected saved outputs, describes the local condition for one insertion pattern, and reproduces one bounded clause search. Most wording in the examples is authored; we do not compare against the earlier systems.

For rendered text $x$, $\norm{x}$ lowercases and keeps only ASCII letters; exactness means $\norm{x}=\rev(\norm{x})$. Write the normalized parent as
\begin{equation}
 p=AqC\rev(q)\rev(A),\qquad C=\rev(C),
\end{equation}
where $q$ is the retained bridge between edit positions. Insert $U$ after $A$ and $V$ after $C$:
\begin{equation}
 p'=AUqCV\rev(q)\rev(A).
\end{equation}
For $|U|=|V|$, the child is a palindrome exactly when
\begin{equation}
 Uq=q\rev(V). \label{eq:seam}
\end{equation}
After removing the mirrored outer $A$, the two prefixes adjacent to $C$ are $Uq$ and $q\rev(V)$. Their equality also matches the suffix by reversal; the center is already palindromic. We separately check every reported child with two full-tape validators.

\section{Results}

\paragraph{Saved lineage.} One selected branch grows from 568 to 752 letters in four edits: one paired insertion, followed by three mirrored-support replacements. Equation~\ref{eq:seam} applies only to the insertion; each descendant is checked separately against the full-tape condition. Table~\ref{tab:lineage} reports all five stages in this branch. They are dependent outputs, not independent trials. The repetition statistics describe the surface and do not measure readability.

The 752-letter endpoint passes both validators (normalized SHA-256 begins \code{109c758e} and ends \code{f5106f222de3c}). From 686 to 752 letters, distinct token types fall from 56 to 48 and repeated-trigram rate rises from 4.8\% to 6.6\%. The endpoint contains rough clauses and a proper palindromic span. It is not a reader-validated example or a length record. The full text and replay materials are in the companion evidence archive.

\paragraph{Other checks.} A separate bounded search adds four clauses at two seams of the 568-letter parent, yielding an exact 672-letter child after 9,273 frontier examinations, 35 rejected attempts, and one accepted path. Its inserted clauses are: \emph{Nora stops Leon. Leon stops Nora. Nora stops Noel. Noel spots Nora. Aron stops Leon. Leon spots Aron. Aron spots Noel. Noel spots Aron.} The clauses are schematic and do not establish readability. In a paired-seam ablation, the complete 568-to-630 edit is exact, while either one-sided insertion fails. The equation regression found no mismatch in 54,145 equal-length cases or 91 context cases.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.} This is a retrospective audit of selected outputs; several share a parent. We collected no reader judgments and ran no language-quality comparison. Human evaluation requires explicit constructs and a reproducible protocol \citep{howcroft-etal-2020-twenty,khashabi-etal-2022-genie}. Our checks address character symmetry and replay only; they provide no evidence about fluency, coherence, or reader understanding.

\section*{Ethical considerations and assistance}
No participant data were collected. Generative AI assisted construction, code, drafting, and internal review; it was not human evaluation.
